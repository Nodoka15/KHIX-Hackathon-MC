# Vegi-Knights

Project made for Knight Hacks IX! Enjoy slicing some vegetables with your friends through interactivity with your camera and hands.

Check out our project demo here!: [link](https://github.com/Nodoka15/Vegi-Knights)

The game primarily uses camera detection for your hands in order to track the vegetables the player is slashing. The game offers a single player and multiplayer mode for the player.

In the single player mode, they have unlimited time to get as much points as possible by slashing the vegetables, but they are limited to 3 lives. Lives are deducted either by not slashing a vegetable with their fingers or slashing a "Poison Mushroom." Users can keep playing to get the highest score possible!

Multiplayer will allow you to connect to another player and play the game with them. In this mode, they share all the vegetables, and they have to fight to slash as many vegetables/points as possible over the other player by the time limit (30 seconds). Stay vigilant! Since each player still have 3 lives, slashing Poison Mushrooms can make you lose even if you have more points! :)
